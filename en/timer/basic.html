❓ Basic Timer — Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online timer free to use?

Yes, this online timer is completely free. There is no signup required, no download needed, and no hidden costs. It runs directly in your web browser on any device including desktop, tablet, and mobile phone. You can set any countdown from one second up to 59 minutes and 59 seconds; dedicated 60- and 90-minute timers cover longer sessions.

Does the timer work in the background?

Yes. The timer continues counting down even when the browser tab is in the background or minimized. When the timer reaches zero, you will hear an alarm sound and see a browser notification (if you have granted permission). For mobile users, keep the screen unlocked or pin the tab to ensure reliable notifications.

Can I use this timer for cooking, studying, or exercise?

Yes. This basic online timer works for any time-based task: cooking pasta, steeping tea, study sessions, workout intervals, presentation rehearsals, or meditation. For specialized use cases, we also provide dedicated timers for Pomodoro focus sessions, workout intervals, cooking, and many more on the same site.
